# World-action model bottlenecks: literature and experimental priorities

Search snapshot: 1 November 2026 (not the whole of November). We opened the arXiv landing page of each of the 46 works below; conference status is stated only where checked. **NR** means a comparable numeric result or training-set size was not verified for this review, not that the paper lacks one. Several papers report an evaluation count instead of a performance score; this establishes scope, not that the method works. These gaps limit comparisons. Preprints are author claims, not independently reproduced results. No candidate was dropped because its existence could not be verified among the 46 opened; this is not a count of every search hit screened. Evidence varies substantially in embodiment, data, sensor, and compute. No cited result establishes the proposed recipe for Tianji/Wuji.

**Main conclusion.** The observed 0.93 real-latent progress correlation is useful, but neither a good progress predictor nor photorealistic reconstruction identifies counterfactual dynamics from one action per state. Prioritize matched action interventions, bottle-scale observations, then closed-loop and multi-step calibration. Do not optimize policy against imagined value until all three pass held-out real-transition tests. Camera occlusion and the 54-D joint null space impose genuine partial observability: actions may change joints without a visible effect, and contact physics cannot be inferred uniquely from one image.

## B1 — action following, identifiability and data

Each row gives method; reported evidence and data scale; and mapping. A result on another setup is not a predicted gain here.

| Work | Evidence, scale, mapping |
|---|---|
| [CoCo, 2608.04653](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04653), preprint 2026 | Counterfactual inverse/zero actions and mirrored scenes; ARC inverse 0.412, drift energy −17.07%, Mini-SSMB; dataset size NR. Most direct B1 analogue; valid geometric transformations only. |
| [Ctrl-World, 2510.10125](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10125), ICLR 2026 | Frame-level control and memory; reported +44.7% policy success, 95k DROID trajectories/564 scenes. Inject per-step actions; its scale and multiview setup differ greatly. |
| [IRASim, 2406.14540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14540), ICCV 2025 | Frame-aligned action conditioning; Push-T IoU 0.637→0.961 with planning; four robot datasets, counts NR. Align each action step to each latent target; metric is not bottle success. |
| [WorldEval, 2505.19017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19017), preprint 2025 | Policy2Vec compresses controls for video rollout and policy ranking; real policy correlation reported, coefficient/scale NR. Try low-dimensional, normalized action embeddings, test actual action response. |
| [WorldGym, 2506.00613](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00613), ICLR 2026 | Action-conditioned simulated rollouts and VLM grading recover real policy rankings; multiple policies on Bridge, dataset size NR. Benchmark paired rankings, not appearance alone. |
| [Genie Envisioner, 2508.05635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05635), ICLR 2026 | Joint foundation video/policy/simulator; foundation data 1M robot episodes, numeric control result NR. Measure instruction/action alignment; direct retraining is impractical. |
| [GE-Sim 2.0, 2605.27491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27491), preprint 2026 | Diverse contact and policy rollouts, state expert and judge; 25 frames/2.3 s on H100, thousands of hours. Include nonexpert actions; speed is not evidence of accuracy. |
| [UniSim, 2310.06114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06114), 2023 | Interactive visual simulator trained on robot interactions; quantitative result and size NR here. Separate action-control accuracy from video fidelity. |
| [V-JEPA 2-AC, 2506.09985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985), 2025 | Action-conditioned predictive features and visual planning; reported real-robot evaluation, numeric result/scale NR here. Compare action sensitivity in frozen-feature latent MPC. |
| [DINO-WM, 2411.04983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04983), 2024 | Predicts DINO features for goal-conditioned MPC; numeric score/scale NR here. Closest architecture baseline; check spatial rather than pooled features. |
| [AdaWorld, 2503.18938](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18938), ICML 2025 | Latent-action pretraining, action adaptation; thousands of environments, numerical improvement NR. A compact action interface is plausible, but latent action is not a verified 54-D control. |
| [LAPA, 2410.11758](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11758), 2024 | Discrete latent actions from unlabeled video, robot adaptation; 220k Something-Something V2 videos, numeric robot result NR. Additional video may help representation, not identify joint effects without action labels. |

**Operational test.** From the same measured starting state and image, safely execute distinct feasible chunks: observed, hold, small lateral changes, and deliberately missed approach, with resets and recorded outcomes. Compare predicted against observed *differences*: signed end-effector displacement in pixels and joint space, bottle displacement conditioned on contact, static-scene drift under hold, and contact/no-contact discrimination. Use action-swapping, zeroing and history masking ablations; report confidence intervals over reset groups. Separate visual response from physical correctness: a model can react strongly and incorrectly. Existing logged pairs with similar states are weaker observational evidence, not exact interventions.

## B2 — small objects, motion and transparency

| Work | Evidence, scale, mapping |
|---|---|
| [SlotFormer, 2210.05861](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05861), ICLR 2023 | Autoregressive object slots; planning and prediction evaluated, numeric score/scale NR. Model bottle and bin separately; slot discovery can fail on transparent edges. |
| [SOLD, 2410.08822](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08822), ICML 2025 | Object-centric latent RL beats DreamerV3/TD-MPC2 on relational benchmarks; score/scale NR. Add supervised bottle slot before attempting unsupervised decomposition. |
| [Dyn-O, 2507.03298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03298), preprint 2025 | Factors static/dynamic slot features; Procgen rollout improvement reported, numbers NR. Keep background from dominating bottle prediction; game evidence only. |
| [EC-Diffuser, 2412.18907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18907), 2024 | Entity-centric diffusion policy; multi-object benchmarks, score/scale NR. Treat bottle, hands and bin as entities; this is policy learning, not proof of counterfactual dynamics. |
| [ATM, 2401.00025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00025), RSS 2024 | Predicts arbitrary 2D point tracks for policy; 80% average improvement over baselines on 130+ tasks. Auxiliary track prediction can focus learning on motion; track occlusions explicitly. |
| [Track2Act, 2405.01527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01527), ECCV 2024 | Internet-video tracks plus few robot demonstrations and residual policy; numeric result/scale NR. Offline pseudo-tracks provide inexpensive bottle/hand supervision. |
| [Im2Flow2Act, 2407.15208](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.15208), CoRL 2024 | Human-video object flow plus simulated play policy; diverse real tasks, numeric result/scale NR. Distill 2D object flow; paper's simulator and transfer assumptions differ. |
| [General Flow, 2401.11439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.11439), CoRL 2024 | Predicts 3D object flow from RGB-D human data; 81% zero-shot success on 18 tasks/6 scenes. Inspiration only: your single RGB view lacks measured 3D. |
| [OA-WAM, 2605.06481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06481), preprint 2026 | Addressable robot/object slots and flow-matching; 97.8% LIBERO, 79.3% SimplerEnv; training scale NR. Separate bottle identity from state; simulation metrics. |
| [Trans10K, 2003.13948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13948), ECCV 2020 | Boundary-aware transparent-object segmentation; 10,428 real images, comparative metric NR. Fine-tune an RGB boundary/mask head on overhead bottle images. |
| [ClearGrasp, 1910.02550](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02550), 2020 | Transparent-object geometry restoration for manipulation; RGB-D input, numeric result/scale NR. Useful limitation: depth completion is not available from this RGB camera. |

Your measured AP 0.46→0.92 at 2× is stronger system-specific evidence than a generic small-object paper. Preserve native pixels in a tracked bottle ROI plus global view; train on detector jitter and occlusion, and measure AP by bottle size, transparency, lighting and split by episode. A crop cannot recover detail already lost at capture; don't infer metric 3D from a single view.

## B3 — rollout drift and B4 — action versus progress

| Work | Evidence, scale, mapping |
|---|---|
| [Diffusion Forcing, 2407.01392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01392), NeurIPS 2024 | Independent token noise levels support long rollouts; numerical result/scale NR. Perturb context latents during training; it is not automatically a deterministic-latent recipe. |
| [Self Forcing, 2506.08009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08009), 2025 | Train on own autoregressive outputs; single-GPU real-time video generation, data scale NR. Unroll 2–4 predicted chunks and supervise each against real features. |
| [Fréchet Forcing, 2610.25666](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25666), preprint 2026 | Self-generated history with distribution and scene fidelity; >4,000 frames after ≤36 GPU-hours fine-tuning a 1B model on 381-frame clips. Interesting but distribution matching can conceal wrong actions. |
| [TD-MPC2, 2310.16828](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16828), ICLR 2024 | Multi-step latent consistency and short-horizon planning; 104 control tasks, 80-task model. Apply consistency on predicted versus encoded future; its online RL data differs. |
| [VIP, 2210.00030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.00030), ICLR 2023 | Goal-conditioned value-implicit visual embeddings; real offline RL with as few as 20 trajectories, Ego4D pretraining. Compare goal distance to your time-to-success head. |
| [LIV, 2306.00958](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00958), ICML 2023 | Language/image value representations from human videos; two simulation and one real environment; scale/numeric score NR. Baseline image-goal scoring without matching demonstrator actions. |
| [GVL, 2411.04549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04549), 2024 | VLM orders shuffled frames to estimate progress; >300 real tasks, no task-specific training. Audit real images and shuffled order; may confuse elapsed time with actual progress. |
| [ReWiND, 2505.10911](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10911), CoRL 2025 | Reversed clips create decreasing-progress examples; 5 demos/task plus ~356k Open-X trajectories; 5× real policy improvement with online RL. Reversal is a synthetic ordering label, not a physical failure. |
| [RoboReward, 2601.00675](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00675), preprint 2026 | 4B/8B reward VLMs trained with relabelled negatives and clipped near misses; robot RL improvement reported, numeric value/scale NR. Generate partial-progress negatives; validate unseen actual misses. |
| [Model-Based Runtime Monitoring, 2310.17552](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17552), 2023 | Predicted OOD/failure triggers interactive imitation; real packing 62.0→70.0% across rounds; training scale NR. Use real correction data and OOD gates, not raw latent distance as calibrated safety probability. |

**Practical B4 distinction:** compare task outcomes or progress conditioned on a state, not joint distance to one arbitrary demonstrator. A successful alternative trajectory can legitimately be far from the demonstration. Train progress on ordered real observations and milestone labels; evaluate failure detection separately. Correct/wrong candidate ranking requires measured alternatives; shuffling actions provides negative *conditioning pairs*, not true outcomes.

## B5 — improvement without observed failures; unified policy models

| Work | Evidence, scale, mapping |
|---|---|
| [World-Gymnast, 2602.02454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02454), preprint 2026 | VLM rewards world-model VLA RL; up to 18× SFT on Bridge, pretraining size NR. Candidate only after validating world and judge under failed actions. |
| [WMPO, 2511.09515](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09515), preprint 2025 | Pixel world model and on-policy GRPO; simulated and real gains reported, numeric result/scale NR. A future RL baseline; pixel VLA compatibility and compute differ. |
| [World-Env, 2509.24948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24948), preprint 2025 | VLM reflector and simulated post-training; as few as 5 demos/task, numeric improvement NR. Small target demo count does not eliminate pretrained-model/data requirements. |
| [VLA-RFT, 2510.00406](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00406), preprint 2025 | World-simulator RL with goal-reference rewards; <400 fine-tuning steps, data scale NR. Test conservative short-horizon candidate ranking before RL. |
| [UWM, 2504.02792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02792), RSS 2025 | Joint video/action diffusion with independently masked modalities; sim and real improvements reported, number/scale NR. Inverse-dynamics auxiliary loss is a testable addition, not a causality guarantee. |
| [UVA, 2503.00200](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00200), 2025 | Joint video-action model with separate diffusion heads; quantitative result/scale NR. Compare joint versus separate heads without presuming controllability. |
| [Seer, 2412.15109](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15109), ICLR 2025 | Predict future then infer action end-to-end; reported +43% real-task improvement, DROID pretraining. Predictive inverse head can retain action information; may encode robot motion only. |
| [FLARE, 2505.15659](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15659), 2025 | Future embedding alignment in action denoiser; up to 26% on simulated benchmarks; real novel-object study uses 150 human videos/object plus robot/pretraining data. Cheap policy-side auxiliary baseline; not an action-conditioned simulator. |
| [DreamVLA, 2507.04447](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04447), 2025 | Future-oriented world-knowledge representations guide VLA; quantitative result/scale NR. Compare compact predictive features to expensive pixel reconstruction. |
| [GR-2, 2410.06158](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06158), 2024 | Joint video/action learning after 38M web clips; 97.7% average across >100 tasks. Scale reference, not a one-GPU reproduction. |
| [Video Prediction Policy, 2412.14803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14803), 2024 | Uses video-predictive visual representations for robot policies; number/scale NR. Test policy features against action-conditioned rollout usefulness separately. |
| [VPP2, 2610.10270](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10270), preprint 2026 | Event-level video pretraining, distilled visual planning and inverse dynamics; +18.5 percentage points real ALOHA zero-shot versus strongest baseline, large video corpus. Useful bimanual context; 14B model is out of scope. |
| [Cosmos, 2501.03575](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03575), 2025 | Foundation video models and tokenizers; numerical result/scale NR. Consider pretrained priors only if affordable; generic video realism is not action accuracy. |

A VLM judging a generated image can only judge what the generator depicts. Validate judge on real successes, partials, misses and visually similar negatives first, then on paired real/predicted clips; measure false positives and calibration. No pixel decoder or judge fixes B1. The papers using simulation for RL are evidence for a later stage, not permission to treat synthetic success as real success.

## Ranked experiments and budget

Estimates are engineering hypotheses for a single modern 24–80 GB GPU, excluding labeling and evaluation; actual model size and throughput may change them by several-fold. Impact is relative and uncertain, not a forecast of percentage success.

| Rank | Direction and acceptance test | Expected impact; cost; risk; evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Measure action causality.** Hold out reset groups; evaluate 1/10/20-step signed joint/end-effector, bottle and no-motion effects; action-shuffle and history-only baselines. | Very high diagnostic value; <0.5 GPU-day; 20–40 matched intervention pairs. Reset mismatch and occlusion; CoCo, IRASim. |
| 2 | **Collect diverse safe short interactions.** Branch at approach, contact, lift, transport and release; include hold, slight misses, recovery and varied speeds. Log commanded and measured joints and camera synchronization. | Highest expected B1 gain; 1–3 GPU-days; initially 50–100 short chunks, 10–20 per stage. A pilot is not proof of sufficiency; CoCo, GE-Sim 2.0, runtime monitoring. |
| 3 | **Bottle crop plus global tokens.** Run detector/tracker at native resolution; cross-attend crop and global DINO tokens; supervise masks/center and evaluate unseen transparent bottles. | High B2; 0.5–2 GPU-days; annotate ~100–300 existing frames across episodes, no robot collection. Detector drift and occlusion; Trans10K, ATM. |
| 4 | **Frame-aligned action pathways + inverse head.** Normalize by joint group, use deltas/velocity and robot kinematics; inject step-aligned tokens in every dynamics block; predict actions from real consecutive latents with masked history. Compare exact same splits. | High conditional on diversity; 1–3 GPU-days, no new data for initial ablation. Inverse loss can encode only hand motion; IRASim, Seer, UWM. |
| 5 | **Counterfactual and history ablations.** Randomize/drop parts of history while retaining current state; action contrastive loss on matched observed branches; optionally use physically valid scene/action symmetries. | Medium–high; 0.5–2 GPU-days; branch data from rank 2. Never assign a fabricated future to a shuffled action; penalizing identical futures for actions with identical physical effects is wrong. CoCo, AdaWorld. |
| 6 | **Rollout-consistency curriculum.** Freeze or EMA-target encoder; train on 1→2→4 chunks with target latents, bounded noise and truncated gradients; compare drift and action effect per horizon. Re-anchor from camera each control cycle. | Medium–high B3; 1–3 GPU-days; existing demos. Over-smoothing or collapse; TD-MPC2, Self Forcing, Diffusion Forcing. |
| 7 | **Task-focused auxiliary targets.** Track bottle/hand points and visibility, bin-relative coordinates and contact-stage probabilities, weighted by reliability. Retain a small global latent loss; tune reconstruction weight. | Medium B2/B3; 1–2 GPU-days; 100–300 frame labels or audited pseudo-tracks. Transparent/occluded tracks corrupt targets; ATM, Track2Act, SOLD. |
| 8 | **Outcome-based ranking and progress.** Keep real-latent time-to-success; add milestone/goal and clipped/reversed progress training. Benchmark ranking on observed alternatives and calibrated predicted latents, with uncertainty rejection. | Medium B4; 0.5–2 GPU-days; real branch outcomes from rank 2, including 20–30 near misses if obtainable. Duration is not success; VIP, ReWiND, RoboReward. |
| 9 | **Intervention-driven imitation.** Run baseline under human supervision; capture deviations and corrective continuations, aggregate with original demos and compare held-out conditions. | High potential real performance; 1–2 GPU-days; pilot 10–20 interventions, extend to 30–50 if failures cluster. Teleoperator correction bias; runtime monitoring, ReWiND. |
| 10 | **Conservative imagined improvement.** Only after real-versus-imagined ranking passes, compare best-of-K short candidate chunks with policy-only, uncertainty gate, real re-observation; later try constrained offline policy fine-tuning. | Speculative; 1–5 GPU-days; independent real validation, potentially additional negatives. Model exploitation; WorldGym, World-Gymnast, VLA-RFT. |

**Minimum data plan.** Ranks 3–7 have meaningful offline ablations using the 84 demonstrations, but observational tests cannot establish counterfactual accuracy. Collect rank 1 and 2 jointly: about 20–40 reset-matched branch groups, 2–3 actions each (roughly 50–100 short chunks), balanced across task stages and bottle appearance, plus 10–20 human corrections. Reserve at least 20% of *groups* and several bottles/placements for held-out testing, not random frames. These are pilot estimates, not established sample complexity; increase if per-stage confidence intervals remain wide. Include actual holds, missed grasps and recoveries where safely feasible. New data is needed for claims about failure detection, counterfactual ranking and final real success; it is not needed merely to prototype the model or label existing images.

**Final validation.** Predefine primary real metric as success per attempt on held-out start/bottle conditions, plus grasp, placement, intervention and completion-time rates. Compare BC/flow policy alone, crop-only, dynamics-trained policy, and gated candidate selection using matched initial-condition blocks and randomized method order; report counts and binomial confidence intervals. Also report real progress Spearman, action-response effect error and pairwise ranking accuracy by stage, and error versus rollout horizon. Select checkpoints without using the final test. Single RGB cannot directly verify hidden grasp force or a bottle fully occluded inside the bin; use human outcome annotation at evaluation.
